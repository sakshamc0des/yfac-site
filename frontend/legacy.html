About

More than a society.

A community that brings people together to create change, build connections, and make every effort count.

We aim to bridge the gap between caring about social issues and taking action to address them.

Youth For A Cause (YFAC) is a student-led social initiative at JSS Group of Institutions, driven by the belief that young people have the power to create meaningful change.

Through social drives, community outreach and creative initiatives, we encourage students to step beyond classrooms and contribute to a more compassionate and responsible society.

What we stand for
01Empathy
02Integrity
03Unity
04Mutual respect
05Meaningful participation

We believe in quality over quantity, genuine involvement over recognition, and collective action over individual achievement.

Initiatives

What we do.

Two strands of work, one purpose: students stepping beyond the classroom.

01

Social outreach and community development

We organize and participate in initiatives that create meaningful impact beyond campus, giving students the chance to engage with communities, support those in need and contribute to a better environment.

  • NGO visits and collaborations
  • Tree plantation drives
  • Renovation projects
  • Other community service activities
02

Creative and cultural engagement

We believe creativity can bring people together and inspire participation. Through artistic activities, collaborative projects and campus initiatives, students express themselves while building teamwork, leadership and a sense of belonging.

Events and competitions

Play with a purpose.

Team-based events that combine creativity, problem-solving, knowledge and friendly competition.

01

Pot Graffiti

A creative team competition where participants transform their designated pots with colourful artwork, imaginative designs and collaborative creativity.

02

Treasure Valley

A treasure-hunt-style event where teams explore locations and search for clues or items, testing observation, teamwork and problem-solving.

03

Lifestyle Puzzles

A challenge that encourages participants to think logically, solve puzzles and work together to find solutions.

04

Beyblade

A competitive and entertaining event where participants put their Beyblade skills to the test in friendly battles.

05

React Rumble

Conducted during orientation, this interactive competition challenges participants to answer questions across topics and compete for the most audience likes.

06

Zone Mile: Run for a Cause

A campus run where students come together for a cause. Same route, different stories. Run together, go further.

These events help students connect, discover their strengths, and experience teamwork alongside the social purpose that defines YFAC.
